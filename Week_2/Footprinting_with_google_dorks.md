## 5.1 Footprinting and Reconnaissance – Google Dorking

### Objective
To use Google Dorking techniques to identify publicly accessible mathematics textbooks and educational resources available in PDF format.

### Tool Used
- Google Search
- Google Hacking Database (GHDB) search techniques

### Methodology
Google search operators such as `filetype:pdf`, `site:`, and keywords such as `mathematics` and `textbook` were used to locate publicly available educational resources. The search results were reviewed to identify relevant mathematics books and PDF resources.
